Chairman
Z.H. Shah
Chairman & Founder

The visionary force behind Shah Hayaat, Z.H. Shah established the company with a mission to make authentic, WHO-GMP certified Ayurvedic medicines accessible to every Indian family. His deep-rooted belief in traditional wisdom combined with modern quality standards has shaped the ethos of the entire organisation.

Abid Shah drives the strategic growth and expansion of Shah Hayaat across India. Sajad Shah leads the day-to-day operations of Shah Hayaat with precision and passion.
